Roman Missal

Daily Roman Missal · Pebble Time 2 · 1.3.1

On your watch

Description

A worn cover. A ribbon left between the pages. The familiar red words that once helped us find our place. A missal can carry the memory of years spent in prayer, and of the people who sat beside us.

Daily Roman Missal brings a little of that familiarity to your Pebble Time 2. The readings for ordinary mornings and great feast days are gathered together on your wrist, ready when the church grows quiet. Open it to today's Mass readings, with the season and the names of feasts, memorials and solemnities. The whole reading library is stored on the watch. No account or internet connection is needed to read.

Bold Large and Extra Large text, red Scripture references and seasonal colors make the pages easy to follow. A little crimson ribbon keeps your place as you read. Move from one reading to the next with Down, or return with Up. Settings is close at hand, with a dark Church mode for quiet surroundings. Turn a page, move to the beginning or end, or look ahead to Sunday or the next feast. An evening shortcut offers tomorrow's day readings before Sunday and solemnities. The launcher can show today's celebration before you open the app.

“The important thing is that we should love the Mass and make it the centre of our day.” — St. Josemaría Escrivá, Christ Is Passing By, no. 154

This edition contains selected English Mass readings for the General Roman Calendar, 2020–2037. It is a daily reading companion, rather than the complete liturgical Roman Missal: the Order of Mass and all its prayers are not included. One reading form is provided for each date with a daytime Mass. National and local calendars, optional memorial choices and separate vigil readings are not included. Epiphany is January 6; Ascension and Corpus Christi are Thursdays. Christmas uses Mass during the Day; Holy Saturday displays a notice, without the Easter Vigil readings.

Readings: Pocket Missal Project, Pocket Missal [v.04.X.2026], based on the New American Bible; John 8:12–20 supplied from USCCB (CCD). Calendar: romcal (MIT). No analytics or personal data collection.
