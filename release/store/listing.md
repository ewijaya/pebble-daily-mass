# Roman Missal

Category: Daily
Platform: Pebble Time 2 (emery)
Version: 1.4.0
Developer: Edward

## Description

A worn cover. A ribbon left between the pages. The familiar red words that once
helped us find our place. A missal can carry the memory of years spent in prayer,
and of the people who sat beside us.

Daily Roman Missal brings a little of that familiarity to your Pebble Time 2.
The readings for ordinary mornings and great feast days are gathered together
on your wrist, ready when the church grows quiet. Open it to today's Mass
readings, with the season and the names of feasts, memorials and solemnities.
The whole reading library is stored on the watch. No account or internet
connection is needed to read.

Bold Large and Extra Large text, red Scripture references and seasonal colors
make the pages easy to follow. A little crimson ribbon keeps your place as you
read. Move from one reading to the next with Down, or return with Up. Settings
is close at hand, with a dark Church mode for quiet surroundings. Swipe to
scroll, tap for a few lines, or hold to turn pages. Look ahead to Sunday or
the next feast. An evening shortcut offers tomorrow's
day readings before Sunday and solemnities. The launcher can show today's
celebration before you open the app.

“The important thing is that we should love the Mass and make it the centre of our day.”
— St. Josemaría Escrivá, Christ Is Passing By, no. 154

This edition contains selected English Mass readings for the General Roman
Calendar, 2020–2037. It is a daily reading companion, rather than the complete
liturgical Roman Missal: the Order of Mass and all its prayers are not included.
One reading form is provided for each date with a daytime Mass. National and
local calendars, optional memorial choices and separate vigil readings are not
included. Epiphany is January 6; Ascension and Corpus Christi are Thursdays.
Christmas uses Mass during the Day; Holy Saturday displays a notice, without
the Easter Vigil readings.

Readings: Pocket Missal Project, Pocket Missal [v.04.X.2026], based on the New
American Bible; John 8:12–20 supplied from USCCB (CCD). Calendar: romcal (MIT).
No analytics or personal data collection.

## Release notes

Daily Roman Missal 1.4.0 makes following the readings easier.

Swipe to scroll the menu and readings. Tap Up or Down for a short scroll;
hold for half a second to turn pages, repeating until you release.

The main menu wraps from its last item back to its first. At the end of a
reading, tap Down to open the next reading directly; tap Up at its beginning
to return to the previous reading. Held paging stops at reading boundaries.
The transition cards are removed, and the Gospel ends on its final text.

Readings, liturgical-calendar selection and offline operation are unchanged.

## Privacy

The watch app collects and transmits no personal data. It stores only the
chosen font size and theme on the watch. Calendar and reading data are bundled locally;
launcher summaries are stored by the watch system. The Pebble store and
companion application have their own privacy practices.

## Assets

The approved engraved-style gold Chi-Rho on crimson supplies the 80×80 and
144×144 store icons. The matching banner uses plain gold corners and a straight,
polished silver PT2. Store banner export: 720×320. Carousel images are native,
unmodified 200×228 captures from the release app, ordered in carousel.json.
The store and launcher name remains Roman Missal; Daily Roman Missal is the
full brand name.
