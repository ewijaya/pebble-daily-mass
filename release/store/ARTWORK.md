# Store artwork

The owner approved a crimson missal-cover design with a gold Chi-Rho, plain
joined gold border corners, and no alpha/omega or decorative title separator.
The symbol follows the solid engraving-style reference with its open rho curve.
The watch render uses smooth silver metal, a plain black strap and upright
alignment. Artwork was generated and refined with the built-in image_gen tool.

- `icon-master.png`: square master; `icon-small.png` and `icon-large.png` are
  80×80 and 144×144 PNG exports.
- `banner-master.png`: the approved direction adapted to the store's 9:4 format;
  `banner-emery.png` is the 720×320 PNG export. The readability revision uses
  a larger watch face and cleaner, solid gold title lettering to survive the
  store’s small display size. The built-in image_gen tool edited the approved
  banner with the actual All Saints screenshot as a supporting reference.
  [Final generation prompt](banner-generation-prompt.md).
- `screenshots/`: actual, unmodified 200×228 emulator captures from the release
  bundle. Their selected order and purpose are recorded in `carousel.json`.

The banner is illustrative product artwork; the carousel shows the actual app.
Exploratory versions, supplied references and detailed generation prompts remain
local in the ignored `design/` directory.
