# Daily Roman Missal 1.5.0

Choose Mass now opens the readings for vigils and special Masses, alongside the normal daily selection.

- **Christmas:** Day, Vigil, Night and Dawn.
- **Easter Vigil:** all seven Old Testament readings and their psalms, the Epistle and psalm, and the correct A/B/C Gospel. Choose the psalm after the seventh reading for a celebration with or without baptism.
- **Pentecost Vigil:** simple form with Genesis, Exodus, Ezekiel or Joel as the first reading, or the extended sequence.
- **Saints' vigils:** Nativity of St John the Baptist, Saints Peter and Paul, and the Assumption.

The Evening Mass shortcut opens the relevant chooser when available. Choosing a Mass updates the reading list and its color. Up/Down navigation continues through the selected sequence, including repeated psalms. Reopening the app or changing dates restores the daily default.

The library remains fully offline on Pebble Time 2. All 6,575 existing daily reading selections are unchanged. Text size, Church mode, date shortcuts and App Glance continue to work.

This edition follows the General Roman Calendar for 2020–2037. Long reading forms and first psalm options are used, with the Easter baptism choice described above. Holy Saturday has no daytime Mass; its Easter Vigil is for after nightfall. The complete vigil rites, intervening prayers, national/local calendars and optional-memorial selection are outside this edition.

Validation includes all four host test suites, complete reading-part checks and Emery emulator coverage of Mass selection, reading navigation, font/theme settings and date boundaries. Dedicated physical battery and overnight/offline measurements remain unverified.
