# Daily Roman Missal 1.2.0

A small companion for the daily habit of returning to the Word. Daily Roman
Missal gathers English Catholic Mass readings on your Pebble Time 2, ready
without an account or an internet connection.

## In this release

- Today's readings selected automatically with the General Roman Calendar,
  covering 2020–2037, including the names of feasts, memorials and solemnities.
- Liturgical colors alongside traditional red Scripture references and rubrics.
- Bold Large and Extra Large text. Extra Large is the default; your choice persists.
- Page navigation and quick jumps to the beginning or end of a reading.
- Next Sunday and Next Holy Day shortcuts, with held Up/Down to browse days.
- An optional Evening Mass shortcut after 4 PM on Saturdays and before
  solemnities, offering tomorrow's day readings with a notice about omitted vigils.
- Offline App Glance subtitles showing the day's celebration in the launcher.
- Calendar and citation corrections, including the supplied John 8:12–20 text.

The app opens to today's menu each time. It does not save reading positions.

## Install

**Requires Pebble Time 2 (Emery).** Download `daily-roman-missal.pbw` below for
sideloading. The store listing is [Roman Missal](https://apps.repebble.com/9e466762014d4b1ab248219f).

## Scope

This edition provides selected Mass readings, not the complete liturgical
Roman Missal or its Order of Mass and all prayers. It follows the General Roman
Calendar with Epiphany on January 6 and Ascension/Corpus Christi on Thursdays.
National and local calendars, optional memorial choices, alternative forms and
separate vigil readings are not included. Christmas uses Mass during the Day;
Holy Saturday shows a notice without Easter Vigil readings.

## Validation and privacy

All text-decoder, calendar, App Glance and date-planning host tests passed under
ASan/UBSan. Release behavior and screenshots were checked on the Emery emulator.
A dedicated physical battery/offline overnight soak remains unmeasured.
The app has no analytics, account or runtime network dependency.

Reading and software attribution is retained in the app's content notices and
repository. Source EPUB and supplemental text are excluded from the repository.
