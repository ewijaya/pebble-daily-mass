# Daily Roman Missal 1.3.0

A small ribbon, familiar red words, and the readings close at hand as we return
to the Mass at the centre of our day.

## What changed

- **Follow the Mass continuously.** Down at the last page shows the next reading's
  name and citation; press Down again to continue. Up at the beginning returns
  to the previous reading's last page. Long readings keep all their parts.
- **Find Settings on the main menu**, below Day details, or press Select while
  reading. Choose Large or Extra Large text and Light or Church (dark) mode.
- **Larger text throughout.** Menu labels, citations and notices follow the text
  setting, with a compact date/season header. Extra Large remains the default.
- **A crimson missal ribbon** grows with progress through the whole reading.
  Its quiet opening animation recalls the ribbon in a printed missal; page
  turns update it immediately.
- Refreshed store screenshots and the banner's discreet “FOR PEBBLE TIME 2” imprint.

Text size and theme are remembered. Reading positions and browsed dates are not;
each launch opens today's menu. The app remains fully offline.

## Install and scope

**Requires Pebble Time 2 (Emery).** Download `daily-roman-missal.pbw` below or use
[Roman Missal in the Pebble App Store](https://apps.repebble.com/9e466762014d4b1ab248219f).

Selected English Mass readings follow the General Roman Calendar, 2020–2037.
The Order of Mass and all its prayers, national calendars, optional memorial
choices and separate vigil readings are outside this edition. Christmas uses
Mass during the Day; Holy Saturday shows a notice without Easter Vigil readings.

## Validation and privacy

All four reader, calendar, App Glance and date-planning test suites pass under
ASan/UBSan. Emery checks cover reading boundaries, Settings, multi-part readings,
font changes, ribbon pixels and animation cleanup. A dedicated physical overnight,
disconnected-use and battery soak remains unmeasured.

No account, analytics or runtime network connection is required. Only font and
theme preferences are stored on the watch. Existing reading and calendar content
is unchanged. Attribution is retained in the app and repository; source EPUB
and supplemental text remain excluded from the repository.
