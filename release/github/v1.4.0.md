Following the Mass is simpler in Daily Roman Missal 1.4.0 for Pebble Time 2.

- Swipe to scroll the main menu and readings (enable touch in watch settings).
- Tap Up/Down to scroll a few lines. Hold for 0.5 seconds to page by 80% of the screen, repeating every 325 ms until release.
- The main menu wraps in both directions.
- At the end of a reading, tap Down to open the next directly. Up at its beginning returns to the previous reading’s end. Held paging stops at reading boundaries and continues through parts of the same reading.
- Removed the intermediate and completion cards. The Gospel ends on its final text; Back returns to the menu.

The reading library, General Roman Calendar for 2020–2037, liturgical colors, App Glance and offline design are unchanged. Large/Extra Large text and Light/Church mode remain available in Settings. Reading positions are not saved.

Validated with a clean Pebble SDK build, all four host test suites, Emery navigation checks and installation/launch of the frozen build on Pebble Time 2. The owner confirmed touch scrolling and the revised reading flow before the version bump. A dedicated overnight battery test has not been performed.

Download `daily-roman-missal.pbw` below or install through the [Pebble App Store](https://apps.repebble.com/roman-missal_9e466762014d4b1ab248219f).
